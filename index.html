A letter from your sister

To My Dearest Sister

Happy Birthday to one of the most precious people Allah has given me.

You are not only my sister. You are the person who stayed beside me through difficult days and happy days. Sometimes you are my sister, sometimes my best friend, and sometimes even like a second mother.

People ask us what the secret of our closeness is. Maybe there is no secret. It is trust, love, and all the moments we have shared together.

I may joke with you and compare you to a jellyfish 😅, but behind every joke there is something very serious: I love you more than I can explain.

I do not ask life for much for you. I only want you to be truly happy.

With all my love,
Your Sister 🤍
